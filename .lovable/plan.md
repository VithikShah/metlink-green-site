
# Shubham Metlink — Corporate Website

## Overview
A professional, corporate-styled website for Shubham Metlink showcasing your copper & aluminium import business and 10,000 MT/year recycling capabilities. Clean design with blues, grays, and subtle metallic accents.

## Pages & Features

### 1. Home Page
- **Hero section** with a strong headline about your import & recycling business, a call-to-action button ("Get a Quote" / "Contact Us")
- **Key stats** displayed prominently: 10,000 MT annual recycling capacity, years in business, materials handled
- **Services overview** — brief cards for Copper Import, Aluminium Import, Copper Scrap Recycling
- **Sustainability highlight** — a short section with a link to the full sustainability page
- **Trusted partner** feel with professional layout and typography

### 2. About Page
- Company story and mission
- Core values (sustainability, quality, reliability)
- Team or leadership section (placeholder for now)
- Why choose Shubham Metlink

### 3. Products & Services Page
- **Copper products** — cathodes, rods, wire, etc. (cards with descriptions)
- **Aluminium products** — ingots, sheets, profiles, etc.
- **Copper scrap recycling** — types of scrap accepted, the recycling process overview
- Each product/service as a visually distinct card with placeholder images

### 4. Sustainability Page
- Your recycling mission and commitment to circular economy
- Environmental impact stats (e.g., tons recycled, CO₂ saved)
- The recycling process — step-by-step visual flow
- Certifications or standards (placeholder section)

### 5. Gallery / Facilities Page
- Grid layout for facility photos (placeholder images initially)
- Sections for: Recycling Plant, Warehouse, Operations
- Clean lightbox-style image viewing

### 6. Contact Page
- **Working contact form** (Name, Email, Phone, Company, Message) powered by a Supabase edge function that sends inquiry emails
- Company address, phone number, and email displayed
- Embedded map placeholder
- Business hours

## Global Elements
- **Header** — Logo, navigation menu, "Contact Us" CTA button
- **Footer** — Company info, quick links, social media placeholders, copyright
- **Responsive design** — works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Professional color scheme** — navy/dark blue primary, light grays, white backgrounds, subtle copper accent color

## Backend (Lovable Cloud)
- Edge function to handle contact form submissions and send notification emails
- Input validation on both client and server side
